How to Apply for Overseas Jobs with NOVA

Because this website is completely static without a database backend, applications are processed directly through our recruitment counseling team. Follow these simple steps:

Step 1
Select Desired Trade

Identify a vacancy category matching your vocational skills, qualifications, or prior experience.

Step 2
Prepare Documents

Have your original Passport, CV, training certificates, police clearance, and 4 passport photos ready.

Step 3
Submit via WhatsApp

Send a digital photo/scan of your passport and CV to our WhatsApp helpline at +977 9851122545.

Step 4
Interview & Testing

Attend our scheduled trade test and face-to-face evaluation at our Budhanilakantha-12 office.
