Essential documents include:
  • Original Machine-Readable Passport (MRP/E-Passport) with at least 1-2 years remaining validity.
  • Updated Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Biodata.
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (white background).
  • Academic certificates and technical trade test certificates (if applying for skilled roles).
  • Previous overseas employment proof (visas/exit stamps), if applicable.
  • Police Clearance Report (Character Certificate) issued by Nepal Police.

The recruitment process strictly follows Nepal Government Foreign Employment regulations: (1) An attested Demand Letter is received and verified; (2) Candidate pre-screening and CV shortlisting take place; (3) Face-to-face or video interviews and trade tests are conducted; (4) Selected candidates complete medical fitness tests and visa processing; (5) Government labor permits are secured, pre-departure training is completed, and flight departure is coordinated.

Processing times vary depending on the destination country and visa category:
  • Gulf / Middle East: Typically 3 to 6 weeks following candidate selection, depending on employer visa issuance speed.
  • Malaysia: Approximately 4 to 8 weeks after bio-medical clearance and calling visa approval.
  • European Destinations: Typically 2 to 4 months due to work permit processing through host country immigration and consular appointments.
