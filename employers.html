Standard Documentation Required

As mandated by the Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE), Ministry of Labour, Government of Nepal, foreign employers must provide five standard documents attested by the relevant Nepalese Embassy in the host country:

  1. Demand Letter: Specifying categories, quantity, salary, working hours, and benefits.
  2. Power of Attorney: Authorizing NOVA Recruitment Pvt. Ltd. to process visas and conduct interviews in Nepal.
  3. Agency Agreement: Defining the terms of service between Employer and NOVA Recruitment.
  4. Employment Contract: Individual specimen contract outlining terms, food/accommodation, and overtime.
  5. Guarantee Letter: Assuring employment solely with the sponsoring enterprise and safe repatriation.
