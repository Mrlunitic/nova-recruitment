Dedicated Manpower Agency

Your Trusted Human Resources Partner in Nepal

NOVA Recruitment Pvt. Ltd. is an established human resource consultancy located in Budhanilakantha-12, Kathmandu, Nepal, operating under the regulatory governance of Nepal's Ministry of Labour, Employment and Social Security.

Founded on the fundamental principles of transparency, candidate dignity, and operational excellence, NOVA connects motivated Nepalese job-seekers with verified international employers across the GCC, Southeast Asia, and Europe. We believe recruitment is not simply a business transaction—it is a life-transforming pathway for workers and a vital operational asset for employers.

Our comprehensive management infrastructure guarantees organized demand processing, authenticated trade testing, strict medical assessments, and transparent legal documentation, eliminating fraudulent middlemanship and safeguarding all stakeholders.

Why Nepal?

A Workforce Shaped by Honesty & Resilience

Nepal's workforce is recognized internationally for integrity, resilience, and dedicated craftsmanship. Candidates are prepared through rigorous vocational screening and comprehensive pre-departure orientations, bringing adaptability and commitment to demanding global workplaces.

In today's modern globalized economy, Nepalese professionals have established sterling reputations in major five-star hotel chains in the Gulf, high-precision manufacturing in Malaysia, critical logistics hubs in Europe, and world-class island resorts in the Maldives.

Our Ethical Recruitment Framework

NOVA Recruitment operates strictly within sanctioned legal pathways, maintaining compliance with both Nepalese labor laws and international labor conventions.

Verified Job Offers

Every vacancy we advertise originates from an officially authenticated Demand Letter verified by the relevant Nepalese Embassy in the host nation and approved by the Department of Foreign Employment.

Transparent Terms

Salary amounts, working hours, food & accommodation allowances, overtime provisions, and medical benefits are documented in duplicate contracts signed in the candidate's primary language.

Health & Welfare First

Candidates undergo comprehensive medical evaluations solely through accredited bio-medical screening laboratories, ensuring workplace fitness and safeguarding worker well-being.
