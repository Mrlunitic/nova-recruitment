Service 01

Overseas Recruitment

NOVA Recruitment manages end-to-end international recruitment assignments for global corporations seeking reliable workforce pipelines from Nepal. We facilitate legal demand letter verification through the relevant Nepalese diplomatic missions abroad, acquire formal recruitment permits from the Department of Foreign Employment, and organize targeted candidate sourcing across Nepal's diverse vocational regions.

Service 02

Bulk Manpower Supply

We specialize in mobilizing large-volume workforces for mega civil infrastructure contracts, hotel and resort chains, agricultural enterprises, manufacturing plants, and distribution centers. Our tiered talent database allows us to supply categorized workforce cohorts:

Service 03

Candidate Screening & Selection

Quality hiring is the cornerstone of workforce retention. At NOVA, candidates undergo rigorous multi-tier screening before their resumes are submitted to employers:

  • Identity & Background Authentication: Verification of citizenship, passport validity, and clean police clearance records.
  • Vocational Trade Testing: Practical trade assessments conducted at certified technical workshops for welders, electricians, plumbers, and operators.
  • Language & Communication Appraisal: Spoken English, Arabic, or Hindi aptitude assessments customized to client workplace requirements.
  • Physical & Medical Pre-Screening: Preliminary fitness checks ensuring readiness for physical labor and international health criteria.

Service 05

Visa & Documentation Assistance

Overseas recruitment involves complex bilateral bureaucratic milestones. Our documentation cell manages each requirement with meticulous precision to prevent delays:

  • GAMCA / Embassy-accredited bio-medical examinations coordination.
  • Consular visa submission, biometric appointments (VFS / TasHeel / Gerry’s), and visa stamping follow-up.
  • Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE) labor clearance permit (Shram Swikriti) processing.
  • Compulsory foreign employment term life and medical insurance enrollment.
